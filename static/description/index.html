Dispatch App

End-to-end freight dispatch management for PremaFirm Logistics — booking, planning, truck assignment, live GPS tracking, stop-by-stop execution, POD capture, and automatic invoice completion.

Odoo 18 · PremaFirm Internal · module: prema_dispatch

Feature Overview

Everything the Dispatch App includes out of the box.

📋

Booking Board

Kanban view of all jobs in the booking phase. Create bookings from an Invoice, Sales Order, or directly. Full booking details: customer, commodity, skids, reefer/liftgate, flexibility, requested delivery date.

🔁

Recurring Booking Templates

Define weekly, biweekly, or monthly recurring lanes per customer. A daily cron auto-generates the booking a configurable number of days in advance and notifies the responsible dispatcher.

🚛

Dispatch Board

Kanban board of all in-progress jobs grouped by stage. Drag-and-drop stage transitions. Real-time route display (pickup city → delivery cities), service type badge, reefer indicator.

🔍

Find Best Truck (AI)

One-click AI truck selection. Calls the PremaFirm Rate Estimator to rank available trucks by proximity, availability, and load compatibility. Auto-fills truck and driver on the job.

🛡️

Truck Compatibility Validation

Hard blocks if a truck lacks reefer or liftgate when the job requires them. Soft warnings for pallet/weight overloads. Managers can override with a written reason — fully audited.

🗺️

Live Map (Google Maps)

All fleet vehicles shown on a Google Map using live GeoTab GPS coordinates. Click any truck to zoom to its location. Active jobs show their planned route with colour-coded stops: blue = pending, grey = done.

📍

Stop Management

Multi-stop jobs: Pickup, Drop-Off, Return. Per-stop: mark En Route → Arrived → Completed. Upload POD, delivery photos, and documents per stop. Cancel a stop without deleting it.

📜

Transportation Timeline

A full structured event history per job: Booking Created → Truck Assigned → Dispatch Confirmed → Picked Up → Delivered → POD Uploaded → Invoice Confirmed. Every event is timestamped and attributed to a user.

🔗

Customer Tracking Portal

Each booking gets a public tracking URL (/dispatch/track/TRK-…). Customers see real-time status, stop table, and the full timeline — no login required.

📦

LTL Consolidation Engine

Automatically surfaces LTL jobs that share a pickup city and similar delivery dates. Dispatchers review suggestions, accept to consolidate lanes, or dismiss. Tracks suggested/consolidating/completed states.

🧾

Invoice Auto-Confirmation

When all stops are completed and POD received for every job on an invoice, the invoice is automatically confirmed and all POD/photo attachments are copied to it — no manual step required.

🏭

Cross-Dock Architecture

Data model for cross-dock hub locations and per-item chain-of-custody events (loaded, scanned, transferred, delivered). UI coming in a future phase.

Standard Workflow: Booking → Invoice

The full lifecycle of a dispatch job from creation to payment.

1

Create the Booking

Open an Invoice or Sales Order and click Book Load. The system creates a dispatch job linked to that document and prefills the customer. Alternatively, open Booking Board and create a job directly, or let a Recurring Template auto-create it overnight.

2

Fill Booking Details

On the job form, set: Service Type (LTL / FTL / Local / Dedicated), Equipment Type (Dry Van / Reefer / Flatbed), Delivery Flexibility, Requested Delivery Date, Approx. Skids, Commodity, and check Reefer Required / Liftgate Required if needed. The job moves from New Booking to Planning stage.

3

Add Stops

Open the Stops tab. Add at least one Pickup stop and one Drop-Off stop. Enter the full address for each stop — this populates the pickup_city and delivery_cities fields used for route display and LTL consolidation matching. If the stop has GPS coordinates (latitude/longitude), they will appear on the Live Map route line.

4

Assign a Truck

Click Find Best Truck to let the AI rank available trucks by proximity and load compatibility. The best candidate is auto-filled. Or manually set the Truck field — the system will block incompatible assignments (missing reefer/liftgate) and warn on capacity overloads. Managers can override any block by filling in the Override Reason field first. The driver is auto-filled from the truck's current driver.

5

Confirm Dispatch

Set the Scheduled Pickup date/time. Click Send to Driver — the job moves to a dispatched stage and the timeline records Dispatch Confirmed. The customer's tracking link is now live at /dispatch/track/TRK-YYYY-XXXXXX.

6

Execute Stops (En Route)

For each stop, update status as the driver progresses: Mark En Route → Mark Arrived → Mark Completed. At delivery stops, upload POD documents and photos. Each action auto-creates a timeline event and is visible on the customer's tracking page.

7

Auto-Complete & Invoice

Once all stops are Completed and all required POD files are uploaded, the job auto-moves to Completed stage. All POD and photo attachments are copied to the linked invoice. If this was the last dispatch job on the invoice, the invoice is automatically confirmed.

Recurring Booking Templates

Automate bookings for regular lanes so dispatchers never miss a weekly run.

1

Create a Template

Go to Settings → Booking Templates. Click New. Set customer, service type, equipment, recurrence (weekly / biweekly / monthly), day of week, and how many days in advance to create the booking.

2

Let the Cron Run

A daily cron job checks every active template. When today equals (next delivery day − advance_days), it auto-creates the booking and notifies the assigned Notify User via chatter.

3

Generate Now (Manual)

On any template form, click Generate Booking Now to immediately create the next booking without waiting for the cron. Useful for testing or one-off early creation.

⚙️

Live Map

Google Maps view of your entire fleet with real-time GeoTab GPS and planned routes.

1

Open Live Map

Click Live Map in the top navigation. All fleet vehicles appear in the left panel, sorted by GPS freshness.

2

Find a Truck

The left panel lists every active fleet vehicle. Click any row to zoom the map to that truck's last known GPS location and open its info popup.

3

Read the Map

Arrow markers (▶) are trucks. Circles are stops. Blue = pending, grey = completed, green = pickup. Dashed lines show the planned route — blue when active, grey when fully done.

📡

Auto-refresh: The map reloads truck positions every 30 seconds. Click the refresh button (↻) in the sidebar header for an immediate update.

LTL Consolidation

Reduce empty space and fuel cost by identifying LTL loads that can share a truck.

1

Suggest Consolidations

Navigate to Reports → LTL Consolidations. The system scans all unassigned LTL jobs in the booking phase, groups them by pickup city, and clusters jobs whose requested delivery dates are within 3 days of each other. Each cluster becomes a Suggested consolidation record.

2

Review & Accept

Open a suggestion to see the list of jobs, total skids, and total weight. Click Accept to mark it as Consolidating — a signal for dispatchers to plan these jobs together on one truck. Click Dismiss if the suggestion is not viable.

3

Dispatch Together

From the consolidation record, click View Jobs to open all linked jobs. Assign the same truck and driver to each. Once all jobs are delivered, mark the consolidation as Completed.

Customer Tracking Portal

A unique tracking number (TRK-YYYY-XXXXXX) is generated automatically when a booking leaves the Draft stage.

Share the URL /dispatch/track/{tracking_number} with the customer. No login required. The page shows:

  • Current stage and status
  • Pickup → delivery route summary
  • Per-stop table with addresses and status
  • Full timeline of all events
The tracking URL is shown in the Timeline tab of every job with a one-click copy button.

Security & Access Levels

Three built-in roles control what each user can see and do.

Role Who it's for Capabilities
Dispatch Manager Operations manager, owner Full access — create/edit/delete jobs, stages, templates, consolidations. Can override truck compatibility blocks. Sees override reason fields.
Dispatcher Daily dispatch staff Create and manage jobs, assign trucks/drivers, complete stops, upload POD. Cannot override compatibility blocks or delete records.
Read-Only (Accounting) Finance / invoicing team View dispatch jobs linked to invoices. Cannot edit any dispatch records.
⚠️ Override warning: When a Dispatch Manager overrides a truck compatibility block, the reason, approver name, and timestamp are permanently recorded on the job. This cannot be removed and is visible in audit reports.

Quick Reference

Common questions and answers for daily use.

How do I book a load from an invoice?

Open the invoice → click the Book Load button in the header. A dispatch job is created and linked. If a job already exists, it opens instead of creating a duplicate.

The system blocked my truck assignment. What do I do?

The job requires reefer or liftgate and the truck doesn't have it. If you're a Dispatch Manager, scroll down to the Assignment Override section, type a reason, then assign the truck. The override is recorded permanently.

How do I share the tracking link with a customer?

Open the job → go to the Timeline tab. The tracking URL is shown in the Tracking section at the top of that tab. Copy and send it. The customer sees live status with no login.

Why didn't the recurring template generate a booking today?

Check that: (1) the template is Active, (2) today equals next delivery day minus advance_days, (3) the last generated date is not already set to the upcoming delivery date (prevents duplicates). Use Generate Booking Now to force creation immediately.

Why isn't my truck showing on the Live Map?

The truck appears in the sidebar regardless of GPS. If no map pin shows, the GeoTab sync hasn't updated the GPS fields on that vehicle. Check Fleet → Vehicle → GeoTab tab or trigger a manual GPS refresh from an active dispatch job using the Refresh GPS button.

How does the invoice auto-confirm work?

When every dispatch job on an invoice is: (a) in a Completed stage AND (b) all required POD files are uploaded — the system automatically confirms the invoice and attaches all POD + photos to it. No dispatcher action needed.
